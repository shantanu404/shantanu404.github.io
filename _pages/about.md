I am a **Doctoral Instructional Assistant** at [Texas State University](https://www.txstate.edu)'s _Department of Computer Science_. I serve as a **Teaching Assistant** for _Fundamentals of Computing Systems_ and pursue research in _Real-Time Systems_ under the guidance of [Dr. Kecheng Yang](https://faculty.txst.edu/profile/2100629). I graduated **Summa Cum Laude** (3.88/4.00, Rank: 13<sup>th</sup>) from [Islamic University of Technology, Gazipur](https://www.iutoic-dhaka.edu) in July, 2024. My undergraduate thesis was under the supervision of [Dr. Golam Sarowar](https://eee.iutoic-dhaka.edu/profile/asim/education).

My research interest lies on a wide array of fields including _computer science_, _real-time systems_, _control theory_, _machine learning_, and _robotics_. My research focuses on the reliability and architecture of such systems. Beyond my academic pursuits, I have a deep passion for **robotics and autonomous systems**. As one of the founding members and **Team Lead** of [**Project Altair**](https://www.altairrover.com), I led development of an autonomous rover system that competed internationally in Poland and India. I've also contributed to **multi-UAV flight control systems** at [ANTS BD Ltd](https://antsbd.com).
