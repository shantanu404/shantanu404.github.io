Shantanu Rahman

Ad Astra, Per Aspara

Dhaka, Bangladesh

I am a Doctoral Instructional Assistant at Texas State University’s Department of Computer Science. I serve as a Teaching Assistant for Fundamentals of Computing Systems and pursue research in Real-Time Systems under the guidance of Dr. Kecheng Yang. I graduated Summa Cum Laude (3.88/4.00, Rank: 13th) from Islamic University of Technology, Gazipur in July, 2024. My undergraduate thesis was under the supervision of Dr. Golam Sarowar.

My research interest lies on a wide array of fields including computer science, real-time systems, control theory, machine learning, and robotics. My research focuses on the reliability and architecture of such systems. Beyond my academic pursuits, I have a deep passion for robotics and autonomous systems. As one of the founding members and Team Lead of Project Altair, I led development of an autonomous rover system that competed internationally in Poland and India. I’ve also contributed to multi-UAV flight control systems at ANTS BD Ltd.
